Since June 2026, I joined the communication team of [Animal Behavior Live](https://animalbehaviour.live/), an international online platform promoting sustainable and inclusive events. This year, the [annual conference](https://ablaoc.sciencesconf.org/) will take place on 12 November 2026 and be themed around 'Studying Animal Behaviour in a Changing World'. 

In a challenging political climate, and because science is under attack, I feel more important than ever to stand together, volunteer our time, and organize opportunities to keep sharing our latest findings and our passion for science. 

One thing I particularly enjoy is interviewing other scientists, and we were fortunate to welcome two wonderful plenary speakers: [Dr. Iliana Medina Guzmán](https://biosciences.unimelb.edu.au/research/beam-group) and [Dr. Chloé Lahondère](https://www.chloelahondere.com/). Below are the two “Meet the PI” episodes we organized, along with a teaser for each interview.
